Delete your Sidekick data

Sidekick has no accounts and keeps all your data on your phone, so there is nothing stored with us to delete. You control it all on the phone:

  • Delete everything: in the app, Settings → Privacy → Delete everything. This removes all memories, recordings, people, to-dos, settings and downloaded AI models, the same as uninstalling.
  • Delete some of it: Settings → Privacy lets you delete a time range, a person, or a single item; Memory lets you delete any item.
  • Uninstall: uninstalling Sidekick deletes all of its data from the phone.

Files you saved or exported to your own folders, and a backup you chose to put in your Google Drive, stay where you put them; delete them there. To remove Sidekick's access to your Google account, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions.
