Sidekick

An AI helper that runs entirely on your Android phone. It sees and hears what you choose, remembers it, and answers your questions, without sending your life to the cloud.

What it does

  • Answers questions about what is in front of you, through the phone camera or a USB camera.
  • Remembers what you saw, heard and said, so you can ask later: "where did I put my keys?", "what did Sam say about Friday?", "recap my last two hours".
  • Sets reminders, timers and calendar events, drafts messages and emails, and controls the phone by voice.
  • Works offline, including in airplane mode.

Private by design

  • The AI models run on the phone. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your memories are kept in an encrypted database on the phone and never reach us. We have no servers that receive your data.
  • Internet is used only to download the AI models, and for features you turn on yourself (online lookup, weather, Gmail, Google Tasks, Google Drive backup).
